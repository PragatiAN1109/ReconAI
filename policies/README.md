# Synthetic policy corpus

**These documents are invented for demonstration.** They are not the published policies
of any real payment processor, bank, card network, or regulator, and they describe no
real organisation's practice. They exist so ReconAI can demonstrate evidence-backed
investigation against a plausible corpus.

Each document has a stable identifier (`POL-FEE-001`, and so on) and stable section
headings. Retrieved excerpts cite both, so a future investigation can say exactly where
a claim came from.

Searched by the investigation service through `search_policy_documents(query)` —
deterministic lexical matching, not semantic retrieval. See `agent-service/README.md`.
